I'm an AI PhD student at the University of Edinburgh, co-advised by Ram Ramamoorthy (Edinburgh) and Mykel Kochenderfer (Stanford). I'm currently an Anthropic Research Fellow.

Previously, I studied math (BS) and CS/AI (MS) at Stanford where I was fortunate to work with many amazing mentors including Trevor Hastie, Chelsea Finn, and Emma Brunskill. I also worked concurrently as an economic consultant with Paul Milgrom at Auctionomics.

I'm broadly interested in RL, AI alignment, and game theory.
